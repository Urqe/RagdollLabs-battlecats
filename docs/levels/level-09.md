# Level 9 · General Meowhem

[Roadmap](../roadmap.md)

## Identity

**Status** · Proposed. An armored commander strengthens the horde and gives blue a high-value target during the siege.

## Player experience

Decide whether to spend volleys on the commander while nearby ranks benefit from his presence. His exit should be followed by a visibly weaker push or a recoverable opening. An excessively ornate helmet and ceremonial swagger can make him hard to miss.

## Mechanics

Reuse direct hits, armor feedback, gates, and frontline combat. Test one visible commander effect, such as increasing pressure in the affected cohort. Aim directly when he is exposed. Eliminate the opposing force after securing supply for victory, with a castle breach as defeat. Resolve the commander as part of the remaining threat.

## Battlefield

Start with one readable entry and space around the commander for aim. Give the starter and stronger gates distinct lanes. Include a familiar support crew only if it enriches the trade-off. Show the commander among the red vanguard.

## Balance hypotheses

Hold the line, then take a clear shot window. Tunnel vision on the commander or treating him as ordinary red strength should both be risky. Target one short commander encounter. Simulate exposure, durability, nearby pressure, and recovery. Check double-counted group effects and safe firing from a gate edge.

## Implementation considerations

Build on validated armor and moving-target rules. Use bounded visual markers and test that the commander effect ends on removal. Test intersections, casualties, and mobile readability.

## Open questions

Does the commander change the choice beyond the tower? Can players see the benefit he gives the horde without a new HUD meter?

## Revision history

Initial Milestone 6 brief. Keep adaptable until armor and special-target playtests show a distinct role.
