# Level 8 · The Great Catastrophe

[Roadmap](../roadmap.md)

## Identity

**Status** · Proposed. Read coordinated red attacks and move the cannon between threatened fronts before their assaults overlap.

## Player experience

Build reserves before a threatened crossing actually gives way. The visual payoff is seeing warning formations become an assault and holding both lines through the peak. Give the red formations an absurdly formal signal flag.

## Mechanics

Reuse two-lane routing, gates, and wood if Level 4 validates them. Aim ahead of the next visible attack while supporting the other lane. Let the red attack schedule alternate and sometimes overlap. Start with both routes cleared for victory and a breach at either route for defeat, subject to earlier fairness findings.

## Battlefield

Keep two distinct entries and readable gate lanes, with stronger gates placed where their routing is obvious. Trial one optional support crew only after the attacks read clearly. Stage warning ranks where they remain visible while the player aims.

## Balance hypotheses

React to the warning, build a reserve, and leave before overcommitting. Following only the largest current crowd should invite a flanking breach. Target a compact battle with recovery between peaks. Simulate warning lead time, overlap, reserve decay, and the cost of switching. Check for deterministic camping that makes the warnings irrelevant.

## Implementation considerations

Depends on Level 4 lane UX and consistent threat cues. Reuse lane state and keep simultaneous effects within the mobile budget. Test overlap, routing, and breach behavior through the normal input.

## Open questions

Can players anticipate an attack without watching the HUD? If the choice is too similar to Level 4, should the encounters be combined?
