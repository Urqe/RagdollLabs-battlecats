# Level 5 · The Pawtorians

**Status** · Implemented. [Roadmap](../roadmap.md) · [Balance and validation](../level-5-balance.md)

## Battlefield and flow

The Purrapult battlefield has one red supply approach and the familiar one-finger cannon. From left to right, aim at ×25, the open ×5 starter, ×100, or the optional Purrapult. A ×5 relay lies immediately beyond ×100. A cat multiplied to 100 becomes 500 when its cohort reaches the relay ahead of the receiving line. Three cats from a volley can therefore deliver 1,500 reinforcements. A retreat that pushes the receiving line past the relay makes those waves arrive at ×100 until blue retakes the ground.

1. **Opening.** Ordinary reds advance. Start at ×5, then alternate between reinforcing and breaking the ×25 barricade. The stronger gate can push back the opening horde.
2. **Imperial vanguard.** At 20 seconds, one regiment enters from the far end, identifiable by broad gold helmets with red crests, shields, and closely spaced ranks. It takes roughly 11–14 seconds to march to the fight on the tested routes.
3. **Armored offensive.** The Pawtorians start contributing when their leading rank meets the blue line. A stable ×25 defense buckles. Purrapult impacts scatter armored cats and wear the regiment down, while ordinary reinforcements keep coming.
4. **Counterattack.** The first ×100 waves can slow the retreat. Massed reinforcements reverse it, and compounded waves accelerate the push to the supply line. Capturing the entrance and clearing the remaining horde trigger the existing victory charge.

## Armored cohort

One aggregate record owns the regiment's finite population, march position, arrival, and losses. It supplies up to 77 persistent visual slots. Its center ranks spread or narrow with the terrain, and ordinary frontline sprites make room as the regiment passes. The same positions follow the advancing or retreating line after contact. Artillery reactions use the shared airborne cat budget and retain the shield and helmet on the affected sprites.

The regiment contributes to pressure and casualties in proportion to its share of engaged red troops. Its melee casualties use a damage reduction, while impacts can apply their full allocated damage. Ordinary blue troops at sufficient scale can defeat the entire regiment. The existing supply cutoff and castle breach decide the outcome.
