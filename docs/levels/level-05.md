# Level 5 · The Pawtorians

[Roadmap](../roadmap.md)

## Identity

**Status** · Proposed. Armored red cats make the player time reinforcements or choose a better way to deal with a tough vanguard.

## Player experience

A hold that works against the ordinary horde can buckle when the armor reaches it. Clear silhouettes and visible impact feedback should show what resists damage and when it gives way. The helmets can be comically oversized.

## Mechanics

Reuse cannon aim, gates, wood, and frontline combat. Test a visible armored cohort mixed with ordinary red cats. Aim at existing gate or support targets to exploit the cohort's slower advance or vulnerability to a previously learned support attack. Keep clear-and-hold victory and castle breach defeat for the prototype.

## Battlefield

Use one entry and a broad enough approach to read armored and ordinary ranks. Keep starter fire centered. Place any support on a visible side with a separate aim footprint. Give the vanguard room to be seen before it reaches the line.

## Balance hypotheses

Build a blue reserve ahead of the armored wave and time support. Late reinforcement alone should be risky. Target a familiar battle length with one readable arrival. Simulate armor share, movement, and damage interactions. Check whether ordinary losses bypass armor or a single counter makes the cohort trivial.

## Implementation considerations

Depends on recognizable silhouettes and a small modeled cohort. Cap armor effects and use representative troops. Test population accounting, damage order, and survival with and without support.

## Open questions

Can armor create a tactical response besides buying a required counter? Is a single armored wave easier to read than a constant mixture?

## Revision history

Initial Milestone 6 brief. Revisit when support behavior has human playtest evidence.
