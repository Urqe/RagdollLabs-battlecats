## Identity

**Status** · Proposed. Fight a final siege that combines the most readable tactical choices from earlier levels.

## Player experience

Read several familiar threats and decide which reinforcement or support investment matters now. The visual climax is a reversal that shows why earlier decisions paid off. Use callbacks to favorite silly crews or uniforms.

## Mechanics

Reuse aim, wood, gates, and a small selection of validated support and enemy mechanics. Introduce a meaningful combination through timing and placement. Default to the trained objective of cutting supply and eliminating all threats, with defeat on the agreed breach rule.

## Battlefield

Use one or two crossings according to earlier readability tests. Place starter and stronger gates along familiar lines. Give each selected support emplacement and red entry a purpose visible from the cannon area.

## Balance hypotheses

Use different investments for different pressures. A single-gate camp or spending early volleys on every support target should give up something important. Allow a slightly longer climax only if it stays replayable. Simulate phase overlap, support stacking, reserve flow, and aim tolerance. Check for combinations that suppress red indefinitely or create unavoidable breaches.

## Implementation considerations

Depends on playtest evidence for each mechanic in the mix. Compose existing systems, bound effects under their combined load, and verify representative win and loss strategies and mobile performance.

## Open questions

Which earlier mechanics players most want to see together remains open. Can the finale remain clear with two crossings, or is a single contested approach stronger?
