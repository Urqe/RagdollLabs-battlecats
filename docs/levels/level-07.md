# Level 7 · The Cavalpurry

[Roadmap](../roadmap.md)

## Identity

**Status** · Proposed. Free a mounted blue troop whose periodic charges can help reverse the frontline.

## Player experience

Choose whether to invest in a charge while building enough infantry to hold the space it gains. The charge should have a clear windup, impact, and recovery. Let a very serious cat lower a toy-sized lance.

## Mechanics

Reuse gates, wood, and automatic aim. Break the crew's barricade to enable periodic charges that resolve at the frontline. Test a red horde that can regroup between charges. Use supply cutoff and elimination for victory and a castle breach for defeat.

## Battlefield

One broad approach gives the charge a clear run into the crossing. Keep the starter gate and stronger gates on readable aim lines. Stage mounted cats in an obvious blue side lane. Red enters from the far yard.

## Balance hypotheses

Hold with infantry, unlock during a stable period, and reinforce after the charge. Depending on charges alone should leave vulnerable gaps. Target a short battle with enough time to see several charge cycles. Simulate windup, cooldown, losses, and pressure recovery. Check whether charges can stack or lock red in place indefinitely.

## Implementation considerations

Build on the support lifecycle and earlier pulse feedback. Use representative mount formations and a bounded impact effect. Test charge resolution, restart during windup, and mobile readability.

## Open questions

What makes a charge tactically different from a yarn-ball hit? Should a charge wait for a defensible blue frontline?

## Revision history

Initial Milestone 6 brief. Keep provisional until earlier support mechanics have distinct, validated roles.
